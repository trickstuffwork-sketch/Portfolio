05 — About

I'm Deepak — filmmaker, editor and creative director behind DeepCuts, based in Mumbai.

My creative journey started with movement. With 14+ years in dance, I've spent years understanding rhythm, energy and performance.

I took that understanding behind the camera — creating content for brands, artists, events and culture, from shooting and editing to creative direction and execution.

Most of my work is shot and edited on iPhone. Because great storytelling isn't always about the biggest setup. It's about knowing what to capture.
